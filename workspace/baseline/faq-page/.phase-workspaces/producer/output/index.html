Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I create a new account?

To create a new account, visit our sign-up page and enter your email address. You'll receive a verification email with a link to complete the registration. Once verified, you can set up your password and profile information.

What is FlowBoard and what can it do?

FlowBoard is a project management and workflow automation platform designed to help teams collaborate efficiently. It allows you to create projects, organize tasks, set deadlines, assign team members, and automate repetitive workflows using our visual workflow builder.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial of our Professional plan. No credit card is required to start. After the trial period, you can choose to upgrade to a paid plan or downgrade to our Free plan with limited features.

How do I invite team members to my workspace?

Navigate to Settings > Team Members and click the "Invite" button. Enter your team members' email addresses and select their role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer). They'll receive an invitation email to join your workspace.

Billing & Plans

What are the different pricing plans?

FlowBoard offers three main plans: Starter ($9/month) for individuals, Professional ($29/month) for small teams, and Enterprise (custom pricing) for larger organizations. Each plan includes different features and storage limits.

Can I change my plan anytime?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your Account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades will be applied at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes, we provide a 20% discount when you choose annual billing instead of monthly. This discount is automatically applied when you select the annual billing option during checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and debit cards. For Enterprise customers, we also support invoice-based billing and custom payment arrangements.

Integrations

Does FlowBoard integrate with Slack?

Yes! Our Slack integration allows you to receive notifications about project updates, task assignments, and deadlines directly in Slack. You can also create tasks and update statuses without leaving Slack.

Can I sync FlowBoard with Google Drive or OneDrive?

Our Professional and Enterprise plans support cloud storage integrations. You can attach files from Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox directly to tasks, and they'll sync automatically.

Is there an API for custom integrations?

Yes, FlowBoard provides a REST API and webhooks for custom integrations. Full API documentation is available to all users, and Enterprise customers receive dedicated support for integration projects.
